How it works

Quick reference

Height sets pitch. Width sets playing time. Tap a stroke to select it. The stamp applies your brush to it. While drawing, scroll or slide a second finger up or down to change depth.

Load starter phrase replaces the score. Undo brings your drawing back.

With the grid focused, use the arrow keys to move and Enter to place or select a note. Use the bracket keys for depth, Space for playback, and Delete to erase the selected stroke.
