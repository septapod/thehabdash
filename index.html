Playing guide

Draw a shape to hear it as a phrase. Tap copper for a plucked wire or violet for a hollow reed.

Higher marks make higher notes. Wider shapes play longer. When you lift your finger, you hear your shape on its own. Where the moving light crosses both sides of a circle, you hear both pitches together.

Drag the cube or the dark space around the drawing area to turn it. Tap the cube to switch between drawing and turning. The curved arrow beneath it brings back the opening view.

Tap the arrow beside the brushes to choose how depth shapes each new stroke. With the five bars on, move the slider from Near to Far to hear the same pitch grow brighter. With the dice on, a stroke starting farther back plays less often. The other two effects add echo or turn a smooth tone into a buzz.

Tap a drawing to hear its shape. The Near–Far slider changes the selected drawing or the depth of your next stroke. Drag the selected line to move it, or pull its rail to change its depth. The brush stamp applies your current sound to that line. While drawing, move a second finger upward or scroll to bend the line in depth.

On the glass, use the arrow keys to move. Enter places or selects a note. Bracket keys change depth, Space plays or stops, and Delete erases the selected line.
